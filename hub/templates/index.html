    Drive

    Hold a button or key to move; let go and the robot stops. Keys: W A S D to move, Q and E to turn, Space to stop.

    Arm

    Each slider is a servo pulse width in microseconds. Nothing is sent until you move a slider. The sliders open where you last left them, in this browser. A joint's first move after the robot restarts jumps straight to the slider, so start close to where it really is; after that it moves smoothly. Each slider covers the limits the robot reports for that joint (ARM_JOINTS in robot/robot_movement.py), and the robot clips every value to them.
